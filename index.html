Privacy Policy — Goal Tracker

Last updated: 9 September 2026 Goal Tracker is a habit and goal tracker. This policy explains what it collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

What is collected

Your email address, when you create an account or sign in with Google. This is how your data is matched to you across devices. If you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture may also be provided by Google; the app displays your name and stores nothing else from it. What you record in the app — the habits you create, the goals you set, and which days you marked them done. Problem reports. If you use Account → Report a problem, what you write is stored and read by the developer so it can be answered, together with your email address, the app version and your device model — all of which are shown to you before you send. Replies come back inside the app. This is the only thing in Goal Tracker that another person reads, and it only happens if you choose to write one. Advertising data. The app shows banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google receives your device's advertising ID and technical details such as device type, operating system version and approximate region (derived from your IP address). This is used by Google to select and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked to consent before any of this happens, and you may change or withdraw that choice at any time. Nothing else. No location, no contacts, no analytics of your habits, no crash reporting, no device fingerprinting. **Your habits and goals are never shared with advertisers and are never used to target ads.**

Why

Your email identifies your account so your habits reach the right person on a new phone. What you record is stored so it survives losing or replacing your device. There is no other purpose — the data is not analysed, profiled, or used to build a picture of you.

Where it is kept

In Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. Data is stored in Google's data centres and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access is restricted by security rules so that an account can read and write only its own data — with one deliberate exception, which is that a problem report you send can also be read by the developer, because otherwise it could not be answered. A copy is also kept on your own device so the app works offline.

Who it is shared with

Your habits and goals: nobody. They are not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or passed to anyone else. Google acts only as the hosting provider for them. Problem reports: the developer, and nobody else. A report you choose to send is read by the person who makes the app, so that it can be answered. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or passed to anyone else. Advertising data: Google. The advertising ID and device details described above go to Google AdMob so it can serve ads. Google's handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy, and by its advertising terms. We disclose data only if legally compelled to.

Advertising and your choices

Ads keep the app free. You can limit them:

  • Consent — where the law requires it (the EU, the UK and others), a

consent screen appears on first launch. Withdraw or change your choice at any time from that screen when it is offered.

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID — Android Settings → Privacy →

Ads. Deleting it stops ads being personalised to you.

  • Ads are not shown based on anything you record in the app.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry.

Deleting your data

In the app: Account → Delete my account. This removes your account and everything stored under it, on the device and on the server, including any problem reports you sent and the replies to them. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Signing out does not delete anything; it only ends the session on that device. You can also request deletion by email at the address below.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to the contact address. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under the GDPR or similar laws.

Children

Goal Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Security

Connections use TLS. Access to stored data is enforced by server-side security rules tied to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but the app collects as little as it can, which is the most effective protection available.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published at the same address.
